## 🥒 Your AI. Your PC. Your Control.

**PickleAI** is a lightweight, open-source AI desktop assistant for Windows.

It runs AI locally through **Ollama** and currently supports **Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct**.

But PickleAI isn't designed to be just another chatbot.

### 🖥️ It can interact with your computer.

PickleAI includes **Actions** and **AI Control** features designed to let your local AI interact with your Windows desktop.

Instead of only asking:

> "How do I do this?"

You can work toward:

> **"Do this for me."**

The idea is simple:

```text
Your Request
     ↓
  PickleAI
     ↓
    Ollama
     ↓
 Local AI Model
     ↓
   Actions
     ↓
  Your PC
